### Planets

The game will feature procedurally generated planets which are created using real planet formation systems like bombardment, plate tectonics, erosion, and more. The result is a large world complete with mountains, oceans, and everything in between.

### Celestial Navigation

Constellations and polar stars make it possible to navigate using the night's sky.

### Weather

A weather system that is more that just noise, it's alive. Moist warm air, solar heating, season, pressure systems, and more all give rise to a dynamic and ever changing weather system. Careful, the gales of November come early...

### Sailing

Ships and sailing are at the heart of the game should you so choose to venture into the unknown.

### Simulated People and Emergent Economies

The heart of the game. Nothing is scripted. The characters in the world around you are (relatively) simple people, they need to sleep, eat, rest, socialize, and find purpose. We give them these goals and a rational brain. Everything else that happens is purely emergent. Characters all find ways to make a living, working, buying, trading, selling, and traveling to other towns. But careful, these are delicate systems, should you the player cause too much of a disturbance you could change the course of a town's history forever.
